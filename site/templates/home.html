How to read a fact here

Every fact carries its evidence. Under each fact is its best source; “All evidence” opens the rest, ranked by how good they are: a record read from the original image outranks an index, which outranks a compiled genealogy, which outranks a member tree or family memory.
Review state is shown as it is. reviewed means a person checked the fact against its source; AI-checked means an automated checker found no conflict; unreviewed means nobody has looked yet. Most of the imported tree starts unreviewed, and the site says so.
Living people appear by name only. No dates, places, photos or documents, and anything marked private in the research database stays off this site. Record images from Ancestry and FamilySearch stay behind the family login; our transcriptions and reasoning are public.
